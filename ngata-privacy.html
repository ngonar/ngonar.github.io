PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: July 28, 2026  |  Last Updated: July 28, 2026  |  Document URI: https://ngonar.com/ngata-privacy.html

shield 100% LOCAL PROCESSING & ZERO REGISTRATION GUARANTEE

Ngata is a standalone crossword game designed with privacy as a foundational principle. All game calculations, puzzle validations, scorekeeping, and board generation occur 100% locally on your device. No registration is required, and no user data is ever collected or submitted to any server.

grid_on 1. Overview & Purpose

Ngata is an interactive crossword game application designed for word puzzle enthusiasts. The application allows users to solve, generate, and enjoy crossword puzzles in a clean, distraction-free environment.

We believe your gaming experience and personal privacy should be completely secure. This Privacy Policy outlines how Ngata operates to guarantee your privacy safety.

database 2. No Data Collection or Transmission

Ngata does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal information.

  • No Registration Required: You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address, name, phone number, or any personal details to play Ngata.
  • No Remote Server Connections: All puzzle engines, word verification algorithms, scoring calculations, and timer logic execute completely on your device. Ngata does not transmit game data, grid states, or solutions to any server.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: Ngata does not utilize tracking cookies, telemetry SDKs, device fingerprinting, or user analytics frameworks to monitor your activity.
  • No Location or Contact Access: The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or media files.

sd_card 3. Local Storage on Device

To enhance your playing experience, Ngata uses your device's local application storage (such as local database, shared preferences, or local storage) exclusively to store:

  • Your ongoing game progress (e.g. filled letters and saved grid state).
  • Game settings (e.g. sound effects toggle, dark mode, difficulty preferences).
  • Local high scores and puzzle completion stats.

This data remains strictly on your device. It is never uploaded to any cloud server. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the application data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

extension 4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Ngata contains no third-party advertisements, advertising trackers, or data broker SDKs.

If downloaded via official app stores (such as Google Play Store or Apple App Store), standard distribution metrics (e.g. app download totals and system-level crash logs managed by Google or Apple) are governed independently by the respective platform's privacy policy.

child_care 5. Children's Privacy

Because Ngata collects zero personal data from any user, it is fully compliant with children's privacy regulations including COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR. Ngata is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

update 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory standards. Any modifications will be updated directly on this page with a revised effective date.
